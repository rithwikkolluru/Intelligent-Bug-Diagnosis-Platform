Title: System.OutOfMemoryException in PDF report generator

Description:
The PDF report generator throws OutOfMemoryException when generating reports with more than
500 pages. Reports with up to 200 pages work correctly. This is a regression introduced in
version 3.2.0 — it worked in 3.1.5. The PDF library was upgraded as part of that release.
